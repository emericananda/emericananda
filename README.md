## 01 / Behind the name

I'm **Nanda**, a technical support professional at **DANA Indonesia**, an ultra-endurance cyclist, and a runner building toward longer distances.

My work sits where payments, systems, and people meet. I investigate transaction issues, work with banks and internal teams, and build automations that make recurring operational work easier to handle. Outside work, I trade transaction logs for long roads, gravel, and early morning runs.

I started cycling in May 2024. In 2025, I finished **Bentang Jawa: approximately 1,500 km across Java**, placing **59th in Solo Men**. In May 2026, I started running; by August, I had completed the half-marathon distance twice.

I like work that takes curiosity, patience, and follow-through—whether that's tracing a payment issue, building a useful tool, or finding my way through the next stretch of an unsupported ride.

**Information Systems graduate · University of Riau**

## 02 / The work

### DANA Indonesia — Technical Support
**March 2022–present · TS Channel since January 2024**

Part of my **7+ years across IT, application, and technical support**, with a focus on fintech and payment operations.

- **Payment investigations:** troubleshoot pending transactions and status discrepancies across transfers, virtual accounts, QRIS, and bank integrations.
- **Reconciliation and settlement:** trace transaction records and support refund, reversal, and abnormal-case investigations.
- **Incident coordination:** connect technical findings across engineering, SRE, operations, customer service, and external payment partners.
- **Operational automation:** use SQL and Python to turn recurring data checks into reports, summaries, and more consistent workflows.
- **Clear handovers:** explain findings, document resolution steps, and help teams move from an issue report to a practical next action.

**Payment experience:** QRIS issuer and cross-border flows, BI-FAST, virtual accounts, fund transfers, acquiring, reconciliation, settlement, and chargeback support.

## 03 / Things I build

### DIRTYLAND — A digital home for a gravel event
**[dirtyland.cc](https://dirtyland.cc)** · Event website

A project where my technical work meets my cycling world. I build and maintain the website experience around event information, registration, routes, and media.

- Individual and collective registration flows, with separate ride categories.
- Event schedules, route information, sponsor sections, and event updates.
- Route and GPX visibility controls for staged releases.
- Registration data exports and category-based participant organization.
- Embedded films and post-event content that keep the experience alive after the finish.

**Why it matters:** riders need clear information, and organizers need a website they can update as the event evolves.
